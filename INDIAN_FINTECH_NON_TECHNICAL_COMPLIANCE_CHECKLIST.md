# Indian Fintech App Non-Technical Compliance Checklist
## Business, Legal & Regulatory Requirements

### Regulatory Framework References
- Companies Act, 2013
- RBI Master Direction - NBFC Account Aggregator (AA) Directions, 2016
- RBI Guidelines on Digital Lending, 2022
- IT Act, 2000 and IT Rules, 2011
- Aadhaar Act, 2016
- Personal Data Protection Bill (PDPB)
- GST Act, 2017
- Income Tax Act, 1961
- Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA), 2002
- Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA), 1999

---

## 1. BUSINESS REGISTRATION & LEGAL STRUCTURE

### 1.1 Company Registration
- [ ] **Company Type**: Register as Private Limited Company (recommended)
- [ ] **Company Name**: Reserve and register company name with MCA
- [ ] **Registered Office**: Establish registered office in India
- [ ] **Memorandum of Association**: Define business objectives
- [ ] **Articles of Association**: Define internal rules and regulations
- [ ] **Board of Directors**: Appoint minimum 2 directors
- [ ] **Company Secretary**: Appoint qualified company secretary
- [ ] **Digital Signature Certificates**: Obtain DSC for directors
- [ ] **Director Identification Number**: Obtain DIN for all directors
- [ ] **Corporate Identity Number**: Obtain CIN from MCA

### 1.2 Business Licenses & Permits
- [ ] **Shop & Establishment Act**: Register under state S&E Act
- [ ] **Professional Tax**: Register for professional tax (if applicable)
- [ ] **Trade License**: Obtain trade license from local municipality
- [ ] **Fire Safety Certificate**: Obtain fire safety certificate
- [ ] **Building Completion Certificate**: If applicable
- [ ] **Environmental Clearance**: If required by business activities

### 1.3 Tax Registrations
- [ ] **PAN (Permanent Account Number)**:
  - [ ] Apply for company PAN
  - [ ] Link PAN with Aadhaar (if applicable)
  - [ ] Update PAN details in all registrations
- [ ] **GST Registration**:
  - [ ] Apply for GST registration
  - [ ] Determine GST category (regular, composition, etc.)
  - [ ] Obtain GSTIN (Goods and Services Tax Identification Number)
  - [ ] File initial GST returns
- [ ] **TDS Registration**:
  - [ ] Apply for TAN (Tax Deduction Account Number)
  - [ ] Register for TDS filing
- [ ] **Professional Tax**: Register for professional tax (state-wise)
